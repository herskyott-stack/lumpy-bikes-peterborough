Bikes fixed, bikes rolling — no fuss.

Lumpy Bikes is a neighbourhood bicycle shop and repair service on Morrow Street in Peterborough. Bring in the commuter, the trail bike or the kids' bike that has outgrown the garage — and get straight answers and honest workshop work.

What the shop does

Bicycle sales

Bicycles and sporting goods for everyday riders — tell us how you ride and we will point you at the right bike, not the priciest one.

Repairs & service

Bicycle repair service for the bikes Peterborough actually rides: tune-ups, fixes and get-you-rolling-again workshop work.
